Is video prioritised over control?

FlytBase does not prioritise video over control, or control over video. FlytBase shows the video it receives and sends every control command the pilot gives. In this flight both kept flowing between FlytBase and the dock.

Video and control both travel over DJI's radio link between the dock and the drone. When that link fell to 1 bar, the drone stopped accepting control commands while video continued to arrive. How the DJI link handles video and control when the signal is this weak is set by DJI's firmware, not by FlytBase.

What we recommend

  • Check the 4G dongle and SIM on this drone (data plan and 4G coverage along this route). Enhanced Transmission was on, but its 4G link dropped one minute after take-off and did not reconnect. With 4G working, the drone has a second path for control when the radio link weakens.
  • Review the area about 1.5–2 km north of the dock for obstructions between the dock and the drone at this height (about 100 m above the dock).
  • When the radio link drops to 1–2 bars, move the drone back towards the dock before it reaches this area.
